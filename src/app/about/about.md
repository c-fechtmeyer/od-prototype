## What is OverDrive

A prototype web app giving pricing intelligence for the enthusiast car market. It pulls from an archive of publicly available scraped car auction data and synthesizes results into an explorable and (hopefully) easy to digest format.

It is meant to be a demonstration of applied data strategy, AI fluency, as well as expertise in data product development.

All code for this project was written by AI 🤖

## Opportunities & Challenges

The growth of online car auction websites has exploded the visibility and availability of enthusiast cars (and data surrounding them) to a much wider audience.

### Opportunities that propel projects like OverDrive:

- The growth of a creator-driven economy around cars and car media. See Doug DeMuro, car YouTuber and founder of Cars & Bids, the platform that inspired this project.
- Growing availability of publicly available data with rich detail, especially for mid-market, $25K-$250K enthusiast cars.
- AI-empowered data handling enabling much more sophisticated methods for parsing and analyzing large structured, semi- and unstructured data.

### Challenges that need a thoughtful approach:

- Fragmented and low volume data is *the* issue endemic to the automotive space and for some makes/models/trims there is simply no way around it.
- Online auctions are growing but are still a small proportion and lopsided towards the lower end versus high end live auctions.
- Vehicle taxonomy is the single biggest rock which limits analytical capability. Auction listings are rich sources, a strong taxonomy backbone gives a foundation to build on.

## Next Steps

Next steps for this project fall into the following buckets:

- **Data volume** - Ingesting more auction results by incorporating more sources
- **More sophisticated parsing** - Auction results contain a trove of data that could support taxonomy build out and more sophisticated analyses.
- **Live auctions** - Bring in links to actual live auctions so a user can act on market intelligence.
- **App functionality** - Create user functionality for following markets, making watchlists, future state email notifications, price predictions, future classic spotting, the sky is the limit.
